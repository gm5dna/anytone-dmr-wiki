# Why buy a Anytone DMR Radio.

!!! info "Source"
    Originally published by Norman Williams on hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk. [Original PDF](../originals/images/Anytone/Why_buy_a_Anytone_DMR_Radio.pdf) · [Source](https://hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk/images/Anytone/Why_buy_a_Anytone_DMR_Radio.pdf) Hub item 1b.

1. Great Warranty. \*Up to 18 months by Anytone.
2. Free Updates to Firmware and CPS if required to fix issues.
3. Free CPS software for Windows.
4. Great support via this [Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1798919343492370) and [Website](../external-resources.md).

## Features that make the Anytone better than others out of the box

1. [Digi Moni](../scanning-monitoring/digi-moni.md) (Promiscuous mode) lets you RX all Talk Groups.
2. [Manual Dial Talk Group or Private ID.](https://youtu.be/riU7-LxvEeY?t=214)
3. [Last Call reply.](../operating/last-call-reply.md)
4. [CDT Scan.](../scanning-monitoring/cdt-scan.md)
5. [DataACK Disable.](../scanning-monitoring/dataack-disable.md)
6. DMR & Analog APRS TX \*\*
7. Digi APRS RX \*\*
8. Analog APRS RX \*\*
9. GPS / Bluetooth \*\*
10. Import Digital Contact List \*\*
11. [Talker Alias.](../contacts-talkgroups/anytone-878-sending-receive-talker-alias.md)
12. [Roaming.](../operating/roaming.md)
13. [Full control of your Digital Hold Times.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHfKZ9sekyw&feature=youtu.be) *There are downsides to setting too high or infinity. 5sec to 30min depending on your needs.*

Many more features that you can find more information on at this [website](../external-resources.md).

## Notes

\* Some dealers might set 12 months as standard, like in the UK. But if it is a fault of hardware, they can replace the radio under the 18 months as it is still covered by Anytone. [Read this Facebook post.](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1798919343492370/search/?q=waranty)
