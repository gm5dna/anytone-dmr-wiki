# How do I reset the radio?

To reset the radio:

1. Turn it off.
2. Hold down the **P2** button and dial knob, and press the power button, and keep holding those buttons while turning the radio on.
3. Within two seconds you will see on the display ’Are you sure you want to Initialize radio?’.
4. If you are sure you want to carry out the reset, press the **MENU** key and the radio will be reset, or press the **EXIT** key to abort and not reset the radio.
