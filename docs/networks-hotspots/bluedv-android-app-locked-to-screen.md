# BlueDV Android App locked to Screen

!!! info "Source"
    Originally published by Norman Williams on hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk. [Original PDF](../originals/images/PDF/BlueDV-Android-App-locked-to-Screen.pdf) · [Source](https://hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk/images/PDF/BlueDV-Android-App-locked-to-Screen.pdf)

You can now lock the BlueDV app so it is always open and on top. This stops the app from closing and cutting your connection.

- [Android Phone Setup](https://hamradio.joomla.com/images/PDF/Lock-your-phone-to-a-single-app--BlueDV.pdf)
- [Android Tablet Setup](https://hamradio.joomla.com/images/PDF/Lock-your-Tablet-to-a-single-app--BlueDV.pdf)

Depending on your phone you might wish to read both documents.

If you still cannot find “Pin Windows” or “Screen Pinning”:

- Google the make of your phone and “Lock your phone to a single app”, or
- Google the make of your phone and “Pin Windows” or “Screen Pinning”.
