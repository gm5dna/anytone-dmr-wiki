# Link to Reflectors

!!! info "Source"
    Originally published by Norman Williams on hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk. [Original PDF](../originals/images/PDF/Link_to_Reflectors.pdf) · [Source](https://hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk/images/PDF/Link_to_Reflectors.pdf) Hub item 33.

Time Slot 2 implements the use of reflectors similar to the D-star network.

The way to use the reflector is to program your radio with Talk Group 9 in Time Slot 2 (add 9 in contacts as GROUP, make a Channel up for TG9, add to Zone). This is the only Talk Group you will need for a QSO on the reflector.

## To link and unlink the reflector

### Option 1: Manual Dial

1. Go to **Menu → Contact → Manual Dial**, enter reflector number 4xxx, and transmit for 1 second.
2. The repeater should respond with a voice announcement, "Connect to reflector 4xxx".
3. Then go back to Talk Group 9 to make a QSO.

### Option 2: Reflector as a contact

Program the radio with the Reflector number as a PRIVATE call in contacts.

To link to a reflector:

1. Select the reflector number from the contacts list and transmit for about 1 second.
2. The repeater should respond with a voice announcement, "Connect to reflector 4xxx".
3. Then go back to Talk Group 9 to make a QSO.

### Unlink and status

To unlink, select 4000 from the contacts list, transmit for about 1 second, and you should hear a voice announcement "Reflector Disconnected".

To enquire about the reflector link status, call 5000, and the repeater will respond with the currently linked reflector.
