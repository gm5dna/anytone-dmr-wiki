# Working a Repeater via Manual Dial

!!! info "Source"
    Originally published by Norman Williams on hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk. [Original PDF](../originals/images/Anytone/Working_a_Repeater_via_Manual_Dial.pdf) · [Source](https://hamradiosouthernrepeaters.co.uk/images/Anytone/Working_a_Repeater_via_Manual_Dial.pdf) Hub item 112.

You use the channel in your radio for, let's say, 2350. You manual dial, let's say, TG 91.

You will stay on TG 91 depending on your Group Hang times settings (30mins or Infinity etc).

Now we have a 3rd party on the repeater and they call 2350. Your radio will drop TG91 and go back to the default channel 2350.

So if a repeater has 2350 as static or a user calls 2350, your radio will again go back to your default channel.

!!! warning
    Be aware of this as you could be TXing on the wrong Talk Group.

Anytone say it is 100% correct for the radio to do this.

## Work around

When using manual dial via a repeater, never use a channel that is static or popular on the repeater.

## Option

1. Make up a channel and use your DMR 7 digit ID as a Talk Group. This way the odds of another on the same repeater using your ID Talk Group is zero.
2. Use that channel for Manual Dial.
